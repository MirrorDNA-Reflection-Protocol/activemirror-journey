## Checked Projection

- no pink, purple, violet, fuchsia, or magenta product chrome;
- jade is the only action accent family;
- blue, green, amber, vermilion, and grey are reserved for focus, boundary,
  governance, risk, and rollback semantics;
- Noto/system typography with zero letter spacing;
- flat primary actions instead of AI gradients;
- visible processing boundary, draft-only authority, and memory choice;
- fixed governance vocabulary: proposed / verified / rejected / rolled back;
- blocked and executed are action outcomes, not governance-state synonyms;
- reduced-motion handling;
- source guard, multilingual overflow checks, and browser screenshots.

## Proposal Reconciliation

Accepted:

- centered, single-composer first screen;
- low motion and standard information density;
- accessibility-oriented typography intent;
- visible focus and responsive checks.

Rejected:

- VPN and privacy-tool category classification;
- all generated colors and shadows;
- Google Fonts delivery;
- exaggerated-minimalist typography and viewport-scaled font sizes;
- negative letter spacing, hover translation, generic clickable cards, GSAP
  route transitions, and ornamental effects.

## Boundary

User-generated media and the separate MirrorProd route may retain their own
brand palette. They may not alter Active Mirror consumer chrome or trust-state
semantics.
