Status: intake

## Objective

Give AMOS bold but defensible language for continuity without claiming life,
mind, or consciousness.

## User Outcome

The product can feel coherent over time without asking users to believe the AI
is alive or conscious.

## Boundaries

- Not in scope: consciousness claims, artificial-life claims, or public homepage
  use of synthetic-continuity language.
- Requires approval: any public claim that compares Active Mirror to life,
  cells, cognition, or consciousness.
- Must not expose: metaphor as fact.

## Challenge Contract

- Acceptance condition: continuity is described as a governed reflective chassis.
- Failure condition: copy implies Active Mirror is alive, conscious, sentient, or
  a mind.
- Recovery path: replace with normal product language about coherence, control,
  and usefulness.

## Bad News / Limits

- SpudCell is an analogy anchor, not evidence for AI consciousness.
- This is not consumer-first language.

## Handoff

Use the doctrine to block overclaims. Do not lead with it in marketing.

## Safe Claim

Active Mirror can assemble continuity behaviors inside a governed reflective
chassis. It does not create consciousness.

- conscious

## Promotion Boundary

This is enterprise/AMOS doctrine. Do not put synthetic-continuity language on
