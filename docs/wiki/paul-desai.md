## Short Bio

## Standard Bio

Paul Desai is the founder of Active Mirror and an independent researcher based in Goa, India. He is building systems for people who want AI to become a durable working partner rather than a disposable chat window. His work spans Active Mirror, MirrorDNA, and the Structured Contextual Distillation (SCD) protocol, with a focus on user-owned context, local-first capability, explicit consent, and evidence for consequential actions.

Active Mirror is built around a simple premise: models will change, but a person's working memory, decisions, standards, and evidence should remain theirs. ## Extended Bio

Paul Desai is the founder of Active Mirror, an independent AI research and product effort based in Goa, India. His work asks a practical question: what happens when a person can work with several AI systems without losing their history, standards, or agency every time the model changes?

That question led to Active Mirror, MirrorDNA, and the Structured Contextual Distillation (SCD) protocol. Across those projects, Desai is developing a local-first approach to continuity: a user's context and working records live in data structures they can inspect and own, while models remain replaceable workers. The resulting system is intended to help a person take rough work through a visible loop of first version, challenge, verification, and improvement.

Desai's public work combines product experiments, technical documentation, research, and source code. The aim is not to make AI sound more human. It is to make AI work more accountable: clear about what it used, what it changed, what remains uncertain, and what requires human approval.
